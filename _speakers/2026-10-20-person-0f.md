Yiqun Xie is an Associate Professor in GIS, Dept. of Geographical Sciences, and an Affiliate Faculty at the AI Interdisciplinary Institute at Maryland (AIM), at the University of Maryland. He also serves as the Director of the International Center for Innovations in Geospatial Analytics and Earth Observation. He received his PhD in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, and his research addresses fundamental challenges facing AI for spatio-temporal data and related scientific problems. His current work focuses on advancing use-inspired AI models with explicit geo-awareness and knowledge-guidance to improve model generalizability over space and time for large-scale applications. His work is funded by interdisciplinary AI projects from NSF, NASA, and Google, with research published in both top AI and domain science venues. The research has been recognized by multiple best paper awards from IEEE ICDM 2021, ACM SIGSPATIAL 2025 and 2019, SDM 2023, SSTD 2019, and was highlighted by the Great Innovative Ideas program by CCC at CRA. He delivered invited panel talks on GeoAI for the committee meetings of the National Academies on Science, Engineering, and Medicine (NASEM) and the National Geospatial Advisory Committee (NGAC).
